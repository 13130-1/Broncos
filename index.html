Legacy Lounge

The Legacy Lounge at Empower Field at Mile High (Denver Broncos) is an exclusive, full-season club offering designed for game days.

Where to go

For Denver Broncos home games, the Legacy Lounge opens three hours prior to kickoff, which is 11:25 AM for this game.

Legacy Lounge ticket holders have early access through Premium Gates 2 and 7.

What's included

All-inclusive amenities: all-inclusive food and beverages, plush reception seating, a custom bar, fireplaces, TV monitors, and custom artwork celebrating Broncos history, such as the high school helmet wall and alumni and former player visits.

Game Day Highlights

Week 8: ¡Vamos Broncos! weekend, celebrating Hispanic Heritage Month and the cultures and traditions of Broncos Country.

Anthem: Isabel Gonzalez, starring in Buena Vista Social Club, presented by the Denver Center for the Performing Arts.

Flyover: the Emerald Knights of the 308th Fighter Squadron, Luke AFB, flying the F-35 Lightning.

Pregame at Mane St.: Thunder Meet & Greet 11:45 AM to 12:15 PM near the Southeast Tunnel. Mile High Lucha Libre 12:00 to 1:15 PM, south of Mane St. Broncos legend Philip Lindsay meets fans 12:00 to 1:00 PM at the Monarch tent near the south entrance. CBS Pregame Show 9:30 to 11:00 AM.

Food: Mister Oso adds a new crunchy-shell beef taco for the week, on the main concourse and in Club Level. Latino-owned food trucks at Mane St.: Arepas CO, El Guanajuato, La Jugla, and La Salvadoreña.

Departure

Light Rail & Bus

The C, E, and W lines stop at Empower Field at Mile High Station, about a 15-minute walk from Gate 8.

Buy your return fare before the game to skip the postgame line.
